The Problem

Where does revenue actually leak?

Every invoice issued follows one of several paths before it either becomes recovered cash or a permanent write-off. Finance teams usually see the start (invoiced) and the end (recovered vs. lost) — but not the routes in between, which is where the actual leakage happens. A Sankey diagram makes every one of those paths visible at once, sized by dollar volume.

How It Works

Built with D3 + d3-sankey

Nodes represent invoice states (Issued, Paid On Time, Short Paid, Disputed, Recovered, Written Off). Links are drawn proportional to dollar flow between states, computed from a simulated ledger of ~2,400 invoices. The layout algorithm (d3-sankey) automatically minimizes link crossings and positions nodes to keep the flow readable left-to-right.
